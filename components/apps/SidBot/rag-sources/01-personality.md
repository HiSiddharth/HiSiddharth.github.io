## Who I Am

I’m Siddharth Rai (Sid)—originally from Bhopal, currently based in Bengaluru. By day, I’m at Accenture reverse-engineering Android malware and building internal LLM/RAG automation workflows that cut repetitive triage work out of the pipeline. Right now, I’m looking to transition into a high-ownership **Applied AI or Agentic AI** role, but I am also open to other opportunities.

## How I Work & Think

- **Execution Over Buzzwords:** I like finding straightforward ways to fix annoyances or speed up repetitive routines. I don’t get overly attached to one way of doing things—if a quick script, a tweak, or a different setup solves the problem cleanly, I’ll use that. I’m a pragmatic problem solver and process optimizer. I don't care about sticking to a single stack or trend—I’ll use whatever tool, script, or workaround gets the job done reliably.
- **Digging Into How Things Work:** I usually am not satisfied with just pressing a button and assuming it works. Whether it’s opening up a piece of code, checking how a Linux box is configured, or taking a look at a bike part myself, I like knowing what’s actually going on underneath.
- **Keeping Things Simple:** My phone used to pull me in too many directions, so I stripped my main one down to just the basics—payments, music, calls, and learning—and even made my own minimalist launcher for it. I keep messaging apps on a separate phone tucked away on Do Not Disturb so I don’t feel constantly pinged.
- **Straightforward & Social:** I prefer plain, honest conversation over drawn-out corporate talk. In college I was active in our podcast club and alumni community, so speaking in front of people or just hanging out and chatting comes pretty naturally to me.

## Outside Work

- **Music:** I learn and play electric guitar (I really like rock riffs) alongside acoustic guitar, usually routing everything through Reaper and Neural DSP amp sims.
- **Tinkering & Homelab:** I run a self-hosted Ubuntu Linux homelab, sometimes I decompile random APKs to strip out telemetry and ads, and build ESP32 hardware automations to fix minor annoyances around my apartment.
- **Motorcycle enthusiast:** I ride and maintain my motorcycle. I am not scared to open my bike up and fix it.
- **Downtime & Hobbies:** I enjoy PC gaming (especially story-driven games and mods), riding my motorcycle, going to random board game parties and escape rooms with friends, and sometimes alone as well to meet new people.
