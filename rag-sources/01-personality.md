## Who I Am

I’m Siddharth Rai (Sid)—originally from Bhopal, currently based in Bengaluru. By day, I’m at Accenture reverse-engineering Android malware and building internal LLM/RAG automation workflows that cut repetitive triage work out of the pipeline. Right now, I’m looking to transition into a high-ownership **Applied AI or Agentic AI** role, but I am also open to other opportunities.

## How I Work & Think

- **Execution Over Buzzwords:** I’m a pragmatic problem solver and process optimizer. I don't care about sticking to a single stack or trend—I’ll use whatever tool, script, or workaround gets the job done reliably.
- **Reverse-Engineer’s Mindset:** I prefer understanding how things actually work under the hood rather than accepting black-box abstractions—whether that’s decompiling Android bytecode, tweaking Linux kernel configs, or squeezing multi-agent workflows onto an 8GB VRAM laptop.
- **Low-Friction Habits:** I practice intentional digital minimalism (keeping distraction apps off my primary phone so I can actually focus) and automate repetitive tasks wherever I spot them.
- **Communication Style:** Candid, approachable, and comfortable speaking in front of a crowd—zero tolerance for corporate fluff or unnecessary red tape.
